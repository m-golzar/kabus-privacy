Kabus privacy policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Kabus is an offline game. It does not require an account and does not send personal information or gameplay data to the developer. The game contains no advertising, in-app purchases, or third-party analytics service.

Data on your device

Kabus saves control settings, progress, generated levels, replay inputs, and gameplay records such as deaths and elapsed play time on your device. These records support continuing your game, retrying consistent arenas, and adjusting future levels. They remain on your device and are not uploaded by Kabus.

Tilt controls use your device's accelerometer to steer during play. The game processes sensor readings on your device and does not send them to the developer. Touch drag is also available.

Retention and deletion

Saved data remains until you reset it or remove it. Settings → Reset progress deletes gameplay progress while keeping your settings. Android's app storage controls let you clear all Kabus data. Kabus does not operate a cloud-save service and disables Android app backup in its current Android build.

Services outside the game

Google Play distributes the app and may process information under Google's own privacy policy. This privacy-policy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process website requests under its own privacy statement. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to send, uses it to respond and resolve your request, and keeps the correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose or required by law. Do not send sensitive information or private save data unless needed for your request.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Kabus's data practices change. For privacy or support questions, or to request deletion of support correspondence, email golzar.mohammad.reg@gmail.com.
